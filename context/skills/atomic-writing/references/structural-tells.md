# Structural tells

Source: [StoryScope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136) and the feature table released in [`jenna-russell/storyscope`](https://github.com/jenna-russell/storyscope). The corpus is fiction; each row is a model default with a direct form in a docs page. The checks and fixes live in `SKILL.md` under `Structural tells`, in the same order; rows 8 and 9 share the last check.

Figures are the share of stories at the stated value. Rows 1-8 compare five AI models pooled against human authors; row 9 compares Claude against human authors. "At 4 of 5" means the value 4 on the paper's 1-5 scale.

## Evidence

| # | Tell | AI vs human |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustained metaphor | extended conceit 83% vs 40%; recurring motif 96% vs 69% |
| 2 | Thematic explicitness | moralizing at 4 of 5: 75% vs 37%; narrator comments on the theme: 76% vs 52% |
| 3 | Extended denouement | 51% vs 15% |
| 4 | Total thematic unity | unity at 5 of 5: 74% vs 41%; no subplots: 79% vs 58% |
| 5 | Expectations fulfilled | 73% vs 53% |
| 6 | Uniform register | consistently elevated: 40% vs 11%; mixed register: 19% vs 56% |
| 7 | Marked rhythm | rhythmic markedness at 4 of 5: 61% vs 28%; noticeable sound patterning: 91% vs 55% |
| 8 | Packed sentences | information density at 4 of 5: 62% vs 36% |
| 9 | Claude's sentence defaults | predominant length 21-35 words: 85% vs 46%; subordination depth at 4 of 5: 56% vs 29%; frequent parenthetical asides: 32% vs 14% |

On rows 1-8, Claude's own rates sit within 10 points of the pooled figures, except mixed register (7% vs 19%). Its widest gap from human text is row 3: 60% against 15%.

## How to apply it

Read the page twice. The first read covers rows 1-5. The second covers rows 6-9 on three sampled paragraphs: count sentences over 30 words and mid-sentence asides, look for repeated openers, and check whether the register changes.
